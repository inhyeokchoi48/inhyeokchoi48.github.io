Random walks on a genus-2 surface

Drag to move by a hyperbolic isometry, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a marked point to center it. The tiling is redrawn around wherever you are looking; press 0 or the default-viewpoint button to return.

Generators in PSL(2, ℝ)

Each matrix shifts the chain …xxx… through v0 by one step (it sends v0 to the end of the x-edge leaving v0). Using the same base vertex for all four makes the relation hold exactly; for a this is the chain through the octagon's own a-edge. The default viewpoint is centered at v0, and the matrices are written in its coordinates: v0 is 0 in the disc and i in the half-plane. They act on the half-plane by z ↦ (pz + q)/(rz + s), and on the disc through the Cayley map w = i(1 + z)/(1 − z).
